Approach

How I work

🏗️
Architecture first
Every project starts with deliberate structural decisions regarding separation of concerns, managing maintainability, and optimising performance. The code follows from the design.
🔍
Motivated by constraints
Static hosting drove the Blazor choice. Browser automation drove the desktop app. The constraints of each problem shaped the solution rather than being worked around.
⚡
Detail without excess
Sortable columns, expandable cells, responsive sidebars, bi-directional animations. Details are designed to serve the user without drawing attention to themselves.
🌍
Domain-aware development
A background in economics and tax informs not just the development process but also what gets built. The tools are chosen to solve real problems in domains I understand from practice.
